Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026 · Last updated: July 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how IS Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use Chappie (the “Service”) at chappie-production.up.railway.app and related pages.

1. Who we are

Chappie is operated by IS Studio. For privacy questions, contact us at hello@isexperience.house.

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • Name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash).
  • Plan type, billing status, credit balance, and usage counters.
  • Email verification status and related tokens.

Billing information

  • Payment processing is handled by Stripe. We receive limited billing metadata (for example plan, payment status, Stripe customer/subscription identifiers, and amounts).
  • We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers.

Service data you create or import

  • Search queries (addresses, radii, categories) and search results.
  • Favorites, outreach pipeline deals, email drafts, campaign content, and related tracking tokens.
  • Optional “your business” profile details used to personalize outreach.
  • Integration credentials you choose to connect (for example Gmail OAuth tokens, Figma API tokens, OpenAI API keys, or SMTP app passwords). These are stored encrypted at rest where supported.

Technical and usage data

  • IP address, browser/user agent, approximate request timing, and basic server logs needed for security and reliability.
  • Session cookies required to keep you signed in.

Third-party business data

Chappie retrieves publicly available business information from Google services and other public sources to help you find outreach prospects. That data is processed to provide the Service and may be stored in your account (for example search jobs and favorites).

3. How we use information

  • To create and manage accounts, authenticate users, and provide features.
  • To run searches, enrichment, brand books, prototypes, campaigns, and email tools you request.
  • To process subscriptions, allocate plan credits, and show billing history.
  • To send transactional emails (for example email verification and account notices).
  • To secure the Service, prevent abuse, debug issues, and improve reliability.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. Legal bases (where applicable)

Depending on your location, we process personal information based on contract performance (providing Chappie), legitimate interests (security, service improvement, fraud prevention), consent (where required, such as optional cookies or certain integrations), and legal obligations.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with:

  • Service providers that help us operate Chappie, including hosting (Railway), databases (MongoDB), payments (Stripe), email delivery (Google Gmail APIs), maps/places (Google), and optional AI providers you connect (for example OpenAI).
  • Integrations you authorize, such as Figma or Gmail, which receive the data needed to complete actions you initiate.
  • Authorities or advisors when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a corporate transaction.

Each provider processes data under its own terms and privacy practices. Review their policies before connecting third-party accounts or API keys.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session security. See our Cookie Policy for details.

7. Data retention

  • Account and billing records are kept while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for legal, tax, and dispute purposes.
  • Search jobs, favorites, pipeline deals, and similar workspace data are retained until you delete them or close your account, subject to backup and legal holds.
  • Pending signup verification records expire automatically after a short period.
  • Server logs are retained for a limited operational window unless needed for security investigations.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures including password hashing, HTTPS in production, HttpOnly session cookies, encrypted storage of integration secrets where supported, and account-scoped data access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; please use a strong unique password and protect your API keys.

9. International transfers

Chappie and its providers may process data in Canada, the United States, and other countries. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

10. Your rights

Depending on applicable law (including Canadian privacy laws and, if applicable, GDPR/UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent. To exercise rights, email hello@isexperience.house. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

11. Children’s privacy

Chappie is intended for business users age 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version with an updated effective date. Continued use of Chappie after changes means you accept the updated Policy.
