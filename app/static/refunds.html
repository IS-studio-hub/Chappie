Refund Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026 · Last updated: July 21, 2026

This Refund Policy explains when IS Studio issues refunds for Chappie paid plans. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. Subscription billing

Paid Chappie plans (Small Biz, Mid Biz, and Large Biz) are recurring monthly subscriptions billed in CAD through Stripe. When you subscribe, you authorize recurring charges until you cancel.

2. Cancellation

  • You may cancel a paid plan to stop future renewals.
  • Cancellation generally takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you retain access to paid features until that period ends, unless we state otherwise.
  • To cancel or change plans, use your account billing options where available, or contact hello@isexperience.house.

3. General refund rule

Except where required by law or expressly stated below, subscription fees are non-refundable, including for partial months, unused searches, unused features, or dissatisfaction after use.

4. Exceptions

We may provide a refund or account credit, at our discretion or as required by law, in cases such as:

  • Duplicate charges clearly caused by a billing error.
  • Unauthorized charges after a confirmed account compromise, subject to investigation.
  • Service failure where Chappie was substantially unavailable for a prolonged period due to our fault and we could not provide a reasonable workaround.
  • Cooling-off / statutory rights that apply in your jurisdiction and cannot be waived.

5. Free plan and trials

Free plan usage and complimentary trials have no purchase price and are not eligible for refunds.

6. Third-party charges

Charges from third parties you connect (for example OpenAI, Google Cloud, Figma, or your own email provider) are billed by those providers and are outside this Refund Policy.

7. How to request a refund

Email hello@isexperience.house with:

  • The email address on your Chappie account
  • Approximate charge date and amount
  • Stripe receipt or last four digits of the payment method if available
  • A brief explanation of the request

We typically respond within 5–10 business days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method and may take additional time to appear depending on your bank or card issuer.

8. Chargebacks

Please contact us before opening a payment dispute so we can help resolve the issue. Repeated or abusive chargebacks may result in account suspension.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy by posting a revised version with a new effective date.
