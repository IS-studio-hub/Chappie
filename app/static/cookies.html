Cookie Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026 · Last updated: July 21, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Chappie uses cookies and similar technologies. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites remember sessions, preferences, and security state. Similar technologies include local storage and session storage used by the browser.

2. How Chappie uses cookies

Essential / strictly necessary

  • chappie_session — HttpOnly authentication cookie used to keep you signed in securely. Without this cookie, account features cannot function.

These cookies are required to provide the Service you request and are not used for third-party advertising.

Local browser storage

Chappie may store non-sensitive UI preferences in browser storage (for example email template/logo selections or temporary search UI state) to improve your experience on your device. This data stays in your browser unless you clear it.

3. Analytics and advertising cookies

As of the effective date above, Chappie does not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking pixels for ads. If we introduce optional analytics cookies in the future, we will update this Policy and, where required, request consent.

4. Third-party cookies

If you navigate to third-party sites (for example Stripe Checkout, Google, or Figma), those sites may set their own cookies under their policies. Chappie does not control those cookies.

5. Managing cookies

  • You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings.
  • Blocking the Chappie session cookie will prevent sign-in and authenticated features.
  • You can clear local storage from your browser’s site settings for Chappie.

6. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy when our practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised accordingly.
