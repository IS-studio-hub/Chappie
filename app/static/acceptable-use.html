Chappie is intended for lawful business prospecting, website opportunity research, and professional outreach that you are authorized to conduct.

2. Prohibited activities

You may not use Chappie to:

  • Send unsolicited bulk email, phishing, malware, or deceptive messages.
  • Harvest or contact people in a way that ignores unsubscribe/opt-out requirements or legal consent rules.

3. Email and outreach standards

  • Use accurate from/reply-to information.
  • Include required identification and unsubscribe mechanisms where legally required.
  • Do not use purchased or scraped recipient lists if doing so would violate applicable law.
  • Honor complaints and suppression requests promptly.

4. Fair usage and automation

Do not abuse rate limits, create fake accounts to multiply free quotas, or automate Chappie in ways that degrade service quality for others. We may throttle or suspend abusive traffic.

5. Reporting

To report abuse or suspected AUP violations, email hello@isexperience.house with relevant details (URLs, account emails, message samples, and timestamps when available).

We may investigate suspected violations and take action including warnings, feature limits, suspension, termination, and referral to law enforcement when appropriate. We are not obligated to monitor all content but may remove material that appears unlawful or harmful.

We may update this AUP by posting a revised version with an updated effective date.
